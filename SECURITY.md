# Security policy

This repository is the public contact point for sfclarity.com and sorted.london.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report privately, not in a public issue:

https://github.com/sfsanityorg/cities-clarity-issues/security/advisories/new

Include the site and URL, what you did, what happened, and what you expected.
Please don't include other people's personal data.

## What to expect

- We tell you whether we can reproduce it and what we plan to do.
- We credit you in the fix notes if you want.

## Out of scope

Denial of service, social engineering, and findings that need physical access
or a compromised device.

Please act in good faith: no access to other people's data, no disruption of
the service, and time for us to fix before you publish.
